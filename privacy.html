Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

About the application

Garmin Calendar Sync is a personal application developed to synchronize Garmin Connect workout schedules and completed activities with Google Calendar.

Information accessed

The application accesses Garmin workout and activity information, including training schedules, activity dates, durations, distances, and performance metrics.

The application accesses Google Calendar information to identify calendars and create or update workout and activity events.

How information is used

Information is used solely to synchronize Garmin training information with the developer's Google Calendar.

Data storage and sharing

Authentication credentials and tokens are stored locally on the developer's computer. Workout and activity information is transferred to Google Calendar for synchronization.

The application does not sell personal information or share it with advertisers.

Access and deletion

Google account access can be revoked through Google Account settings. Locally stored authentication tokens can be deleted from the developer's computer.
